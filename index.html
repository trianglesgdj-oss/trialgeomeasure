📚 Lessons

1. Solids and Nets

A solid is a three-dimensional object that has length, width, and height. A net is a flat pattern that can be folded to form a solid.

2. Speed, Distance, and Time

Speed tells us how fast an object moves over a certain distance and amount of time.

3. Composite Area

A composite figure is a shape made up of two or more simple geometric shapes.

4. Surface Area

Surface area is the total area of all the surfaces of a three-dimensional object.

5. Pie Graph

A pie graph represents data using sections of a circle. Each section represents a part of the whole.

🧮 Formula Guide

Topic Formula
Rectangle Area A = l × w
Triangle Area A = ½ × b × h
Circle Area A = πr²
Rectangular Prism Volume V = l × w × h
Speed S = d ÷ t
Distance d = S × t
Time t = d ÷ S
